## What you get

Each page becomes an image, and the images are always packed into `pages.zip`, even when the PDF has a single page. PDF to JPG still hands you a lone image for a one-page file. Use ZIP when you want one download either way.

## How to convert PDF pages to a zip

1. Open **PDF to ZIP**.
2. Upload the PDF.
3. Choose **JPG** or **PNG**, and a DPI (150 is a good default; 300 is for print and uses more memory).
4. Click **Create ZIP** and download `pages.zip`.

## Limits

The tool uses the same page cap as the other canvas tools (750 pages) and the heavy file-size cap (150 MB). A renamed file that is not a PDF is rejected before any rendering starts.

## Privacy

Pages are drawn in your browser. The zip is built there too. Nothing is uploaded.
