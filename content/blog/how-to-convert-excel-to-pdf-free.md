## First sheet only

This tool reads `.xlsx` and `.xls` and exports **the first sheet**. If the workbook has more sheets, the PDF title says so. To convert a different sheet, move it to the front in Excel, or save that sheet as CSV and use CSV to PDF.

Merged cells keep their value in the top-left cell. The header row is bold and cells have borders. Excel fonts, colors, and real merged spans are not copied.

## How to convert Excel to PDF

1. Open **Excel to PDF**.
2. Upload the workbook.
3. Click **Convert to PDF** and download `spreadsheet.pdf`.

## Privacy

The workbook is read in your browser with SheetJS. It is not uploaded.
