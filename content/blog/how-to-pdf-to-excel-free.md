## Best effort

The tool reads where each piece of text sits on the page and guesses rows and columns from how it lines up. One sheet is made per page.

- Clean grid-style tables usually come out well.
- Merged cells, wrapped cells, and tables without clear gaps may need hand fixing.
- Scanned PDFs have no text to read. Run **OCR PDF** first.

## How to convert

1. Open **PDF to Excel** and add your PDF.
2. Click **Convert to Excel**.
3. Open the .xlsx and check the numbers against the PDF.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
