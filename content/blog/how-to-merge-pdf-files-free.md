## What does merging PDFs mean?

Merging PDFs means combining two or more separate PDF files into a single document. Whether you scanned a multi-page contract in batches, received invoices from different sources, or want to collate research papers, merging creates one cohesive file that is easier to share, print, and archive.

## Why merge PDFs for free: without uploading?

Most online PDF mergers upload your files to a server. That means:

- **Privacy risk**: Your documents, contracts, financial statements, personal IDs, sit on a stranger's server.
- **File size limits**: Free tiers often cap uploads at 5–10 MB per file.
- **Watermarks**: Many free tools stamp their logo on your output.

Our Merge PDF tool runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.

## Step-by-step: how to merge PDF files free

1. Open the **Merge PDF** tool on this site.
2. Click **Select files** or drag your PDFs onto the drop zone. You can add as many files as you need.
3. **Drag the file cards** to set the order you want in the final document.
4. Click **Merge PDFs** and watch the real-time progress bar.
5. Click **Download** to save your merged PDF.

That's it. No email required. No account. No watermark.

## Tips for better results

- **Password-protected PDFs**: Remove the password first (open it in a PDF viewer, enter the password, and save a copy without one), then merge.
- **Large files**: The tool handles files up to 300 MB per file (600 MB total). For very large scans, try [compressing first](/compress-pdf).
- **Page order**: Drag file cards before merging, what you see is what you get.
