## How it works

Allow the camera, capture each page, and build one PDF. On a phone the back camera is used by default.

## Auto-crop

It trims the background around a bright page. It does **not** straighten tilted or curved pages; hold the camera square above the page.

## If the camera is blocked

You will see a message and can choose photos from your device instead.

## Tips

- Use good light and a dark background.
- Run **OCR PDF** afterwards to make the text searchable.

## Privacy

Frames stay in this tab. Nothing is uploaded.
