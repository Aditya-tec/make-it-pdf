## What you get

Each slide becomes one page with its text and pictures. Exact positioning is **approximate**: the tool rebuilds slides from the file's text boxes and images, it does not run PowerPoint's layout engine.

## What is left out

- Animations, transitions, and speaker notes
- Charts, SmartArt, and embedded video
- EMF, WMF, and SVG pictures
- Only `.pptx` files; old `.ppt` is not supported

## How to convert

1. Open **PowerPoint to PDF** and add your .pptx.
2. Check the preview.
3. Click **Save as PDF (Print)** and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination, in landscape.

## Privacy

The file is read and sanitized on your device. Script, style, and remote content in the file are stripped, and nothing is uploaded.
