## How it works

Click text on the page and type a replacement. The tool draws a white box over the old text and writes the new text on top in Helvetica, Times, or Courier.

## Know the limits

- The original font, spacing, and kerning are **not** preserved.
- Only Latin letters, digits, and common punctuation are supported for new text.
- **The old text stays in the file underneath.** It can still be copied or searched. To really remove text, use **Redact PDF**.

## Privacy

Edits are applied on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
