## What an EPUB is here

A zip of XHTML plus a small manifest. Each PDF page becomes a section of extracted text. Images, columns, and the original line layout are not copied.

## Scanned PDFs

If the PDF is image-only, the tool stops and points you at OCR PDF, the same check Extract Text uses. An EPUB of nothing is not a useful ebook.

## How to convert PDF to EPUB

1. Open **PDF to EPUB**.
2. Upload a text-based PDF.
3. Click **Convert to EPUB** and download `book.epub`.
4. Open it in an ebook reader.

## Privacy

Text is extracted and zipped on your device. The PDF is not uploaded.
