## What it is

A small cash-counter tool: save products, build a cart, get a receipt PDF in 80 mm or 58 mm thermal width, or A4. It is separate from a full invoice generator.

## GST, simplified

Each line has one GST rate, split into CGST and SGST (or IGST for inter-state). Prices can include or exclude GST. There are no HSN codes, GSTIN checks, or cess. Check it against your accountant's rules before relying on it.

## Your data

The product list lives in this browser's local storage and goes nowhere. Clearing site data deletes it.

## Privacy

Receipts are built on your device.
