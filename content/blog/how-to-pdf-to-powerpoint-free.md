## Read this first

Each PDF page becomes **one picture on one slide**. The text is not editable in PowerPoint. It looks like your PDF, and it is good for presenting, not for revising.

If you want editable text, use PDF to Word instead.

## How to convert

1. Open **PDF to PowerPoint** and add your PDF.
2. Click **Convert to PowerPoint**.
3. Download the .pptx.

## Privacy

Pages are drawn and packed into the .pptx on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
