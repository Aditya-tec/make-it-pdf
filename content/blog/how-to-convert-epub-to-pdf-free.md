## What is supported

**EPUB only.** MOBI and AZW3 are not supported. DRM-protected books are refused with a message, not half-converted.

## What changes

Chapters are printed in reading order with plain styling. Raster images are kept. The book's own fonts, custom layout, and SVG covers are not.

## How to convert

1. Open **eBook to PDF** and add your .epub.
2. Check the preview.
3. Click **Save as PDF (Print)** and choose "Save as PDF".

## Privacy

The book is unpacked and cleaned on your device. Scripts and remote content are stripped. Nothing is uploaded.
