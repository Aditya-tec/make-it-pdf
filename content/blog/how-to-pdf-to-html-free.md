## What you get

A single .html file where every line of text is real text you can select, copy, and search, placed where it sits on the PDF page.

## What you do not get

- Images and vector graphics are not included.
- Fonts are approximated by generic families.
- Scanned PDFs have no text. Run **OCR PDF** first.

The file carries a policy that blocks scripts and outside content.

## How to convert

1. Open **PDF to HTML** and add your PDF.
2. Click **Convert to HTML** and try selecting text in the preview.
3. Download the .html.

## Privacy

Nothing is uploaded.
