## How the table is built

The first row is the column header and repeats at the top of each page. Rows continue onto new A4 pages. A 10,000-row file does not become one giant page.

Quoted fields are one cell, including commas and a doubled quote inside the quotes.

## How to convert CSV to PDF

1. Open **CSV to PDF**.
2. Upload a `.csv` file.
3. Click **Convert to PDF** and download `table.pdf`.

## Limits

Up to 20,000 rows and 40 columns. Wider or longer files are refused with a clear message so the tab does not freeze. Characters the PDF font cannot draw are replaced.

## Privacy

Parsing and drawing happen in your browser. The CSV is not uploaded.
