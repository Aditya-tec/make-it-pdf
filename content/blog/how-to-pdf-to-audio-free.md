## Listen only

This tool reads the PDF aloud in your tab. There is **no audio file to download**: browsers do not reliably let a page record speech.

## Voices

Only voices installed on your device are used, so the text is not sent to an online speech service. If none are installed, the tool says so.

## Tips

- Scanned PDFs have no text. Run **OCR PDF** first.
- Use the speed slider to go faster or slower.

## Privacy

Text is extracted on your device and spoken locally.
